Our Story

How CofeLang came to be

The Beginning

CofeLang started as a small dream among IT students who wanted to create a space where their fellow students could enjoy quality coffee and snacks without worrying about their budget. Founded in 2026, the name "CofeLang" is a combination of "Coffee" and "Lang" (a Filipino term for conversation), symbolizing our belief that every cup of coffee brings people together.

What began as a small stall near the university campus quickly grew into a beloved cafe known for its warm atmosphere, friendly staff, and affordable menu. Today, CofeLang serves hundreds of students daily, providing them with the perfect environment to study, socialize, and recharge.

What We Stand For

At CofeLang, we believe that every student deserves access to quality beverages and a comfortable space to thrive. Our cafe is more than just a place to grab coffee — it's a community hub where friendships are formed, ideas are born, and memories are made.

We are committed to maintaining a safe, clean, and inclusive environment where every student feels welcome. Our team is passionate about delivering excellent service and continuously improving our menu to cater to diverse tastes and preferences.
