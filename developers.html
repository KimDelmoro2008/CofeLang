Our Team

We are IT students and proud co-founders of CofeLang. As part of our academic project and real-world preparation, we designed and developed this website from scratch using only HTML5 and CSS3. Our goal is to bring CofeLang's offerings to the digital world and make it easier for students to browse and order their favorite drinks.
