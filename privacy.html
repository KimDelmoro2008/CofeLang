What we collect

The CofeLang website has no server or database (this is a prototype). Anything you type into the cart, checkout, or contact forms stays in your own browser.

What is stored on your device

Your cart, your order details (name, phone number, order type, notes, items and total) and your order history are saved in your browser's localStorage so the pages can show them again. We cannot see this data. You can remove it with the Clear Cart and Clear Order History buttons, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Demo login

The Login page saves the email you enter in your browser's localStorage so the site can show the right menu links. There is no password, nothing is verified and nothing is sent to a server.

Contact form

The contact form on the About page is a demo. Messages are not sent or saved.

Third-party content

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map. Google may receive your IP address and browser details when those load. We do not set our own cookies.
